## Open items from the audit, in priority order

1. Decide whether business owners should be scoped below tenant-wide `request:read` and
   `finding:read`. The portal lists only their own items, but a hand-typed workspace URL still
   reads other records. See the "Known limitation" section in client-portal.md.
2. Nine routes lack `@RequirePermission`: collaboration tasks and comments, notifications, and
   search. Notification mutations write no audit trail rows.
3. Hand-rolled status transitions bypass `WorkflowService` in library, programmes, timesheets
   and review notes; several modules accept free-form `status` from DTOs.
4. RLS has no policy on `RolePermission`, `LibraryItemFrameworkRef` and the implicit
   many-to-many tables; RLS is not forced, so the app role bypasses it (documented).
5. Continuous monitoring has no engine: rules and connectors are never executed.
6. AI copilot returns canned rule-pack text unless `AI_ENABLED` and a provider are set.
7. Web: no tests, no route-level authorisation, no `error.tsx` or `loading.tsx`, required
   fields not announced to assistive tech, keyboard-inaccessible clear buttons in the pickers,
   no skip link in the workspace shell (the portal shell has one).
8. Docs: contract lacks the AI context-document and alert-detail routes; ERD omits
   `LibraryItemFrameworkRef`; `tenant:manage` permission is defined but unused.

## Conventions to keep

Follow CLAUDE.md. In particular: commit only when asked, tenant-scoped Prisma client
everywhere outside auth and provisioning, every status change through `WorkflowService`, an
`AuditTrail` row for every mutation, `@RequirePermission()` on every route.
